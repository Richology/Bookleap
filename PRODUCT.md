# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BookLeap is for Chinese readers who want to turn serious books into usable thinking material. The primary context is a desktop reading and analysis workspace: users upload or choose a book, inspect structured outputs, export useful material, and practice decisions based on what they read.

## Product Purpose

BookLeap turns a good book into a clearer decision process. It should help users move from passive reading to structured understanding, reuse, training, and expression. Success means a user can quickly start with one book, understand what the workspace can do, and keep returning to past reading objects.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, and bookish. The product should feel like a calm reading desk with enough intelligence under the surface, not a generic AI chat tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS pages, colorful gradient hype, chatbot-first interfaces, decorative motion, and visual noise that competes with reading. Avoid making mobile the primary design target; desktop workspace usability leads.

## Design Principles

- Put the next reading action in the user's path.
- Make books feel like durable workspace objects, not disposable prompts.
- Keep the interface calm enough for long reading sessions.
- Use motion and color to clarify state, not to decorate.
- Prioritize PC workspace ergonomics, while keeping mobile usable and coherent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical production accessibility: clear focus states, readable contrast, keyboard-reachable primary actions, reduced unnecessary motion, and responsive layouts that preserve task order. Desktop use is primary; mobile should remain legible and navigable without becoming the lead design constraint.
